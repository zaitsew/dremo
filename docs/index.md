# Dremo

**An ad-free, open-source calm screen for Android TV and Google TV.**

When the TV is on and nobody is watching, Dremo shows a big clock or a slow
ambience scene, with a soft sound that the app makes itself.

- **Clocks.** Digital, flip, minimal analog and a word clock, with the date and an optional weather line.
- **Six ambience scenes.** Rain on a window, a fireplace, an aurora, a night sky, ocean waves and a lava lamp.
- **Sound made on the TV.** White, pink and brown noise, rain, fire and ocean, with no audio files.
- **Sleep timer.** 15 to 90 minutes: the sound fades and the screen becomes a dim clock.
- **Kind to the panel.** Night dimming and a pixel shift every minute.
- **Screensaver.** Dremo also works as the Android screensaver.
- **Made for the sofa.** Full remote control (D-pad) support, large type, English, Spanish and Russian.
- **No ads. No accounts. No tracking.** The only network traffic is the optional weather from Open-Meteo.
